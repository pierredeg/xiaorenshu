Privacy Policy — Xiaorenshu

Effective date: November 9, 2026

App: Xiaorenshu (小人书), for iPhone

Publisher: Pierre Degeorges

Contact: pierredegeorges+xiaorenshu@gmail.com

In short

Xiaorenshu does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It works entirely on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the app saves two things locally on your iPhone, using the standard storage provided by iOS:

  • which dialogues and stories you have finished, and
  • the words you chose to save.

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app, and you can clear it at any time from within the app (remove a saved word, or mark a dialogue as not done).

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, device identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. The app does not connect to the internet: all texts, illustrations and audio are included in the app.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data, and no advertising or analytics service. The voices were generated in advance with Microsoft Azure text-to-speech and are bundled in the app as audio files; the app itself never contacts Microsoft.

If you have chosen to share analytics or crash data with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated information through App Store Connect. We cannot identify you from it.

Children

Xiaorenshu is a general language-learning app. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with data protection rights, you can still contact us at the address above with any question.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add an account or a feature that connects to the internet), we will update this policy and the app's privacy information on the App Store before the change takes effect. The effective date above shows when it was last updated.
